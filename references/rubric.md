# The rubric (10 points, half-point steps)

Accuracy is scored by the Source Auditor (less any accepted Skeptic findings), criteria 2 to 6 by the Editor, criterion 7 by the Profile Checker. **9.0 or more in two consecutive clean rounds** (see `council.md`, section 7) is the default bar for "a careful editor would publish this without changes". A site profile can raise it.

## Hard gates (fail whatever the score)

- Any claim CONTRADICTED by a source and not fixed.
- Any quotation not word for word on its source page.
- Any claim with no source (UNSOURCED) or not in its cited source (NOT FOUND) left in the text.
- Any hard rule in the site profile broken.

## Criteria

| # | Criterion | Max | Full marks need |
|---|---|---:|---|
| 1 | **Accuracy and sourcing** | 2.5 | Every claim supported by a listed source, in the place cited. Nothing stated beyond what the source says. Where sources differ or a claim rests on one source, the text says so. No invented quotes, numbers or dates. Outside sources do not contradict it |
| 2 | **Search fit** | 1.5 | Title, description, quick answer and headings match what people type for the main phrase and its close variants. The main question answered in the first 60 words. Headings are real questions |
| 3 | **Citability** | 1.5 | Each section opens with a bold, self-contained answer naming its subject. Key facts and FAQ answers stand alone. Specific numbers, names and references |
| 4 | **Information gain** | 1.5 | At least two things a reader would not get from the top results or the main encyclopedia entry: a primary-source detail, a sourced correction of a common belief, a local or language angle, a current-year fact checked in two places |
| 5 | **Readability** | 1.0 | Plain language a 15-year-old can follow. Short sentences. No filler or stock phrases. No em dashes. Specialist and foreign terms explained on first use |
| 6 | **Structure and completeness** | 1.0 | Covers the searcher's questions in a sensible order, without repetition. Nothing important missing, nothing off-topic. Length suits the topic |
| 7 | **Site-profile compliance and call to action** | 1.0 | The site's call to action is earned, described in the product's own words, and claims no link the product page does not show. All profile rules kept. Respectful of belief while honest about evidence |

## How to deduct

- Half points only, and only where a listed defect justifies it. Say which defect costs which half point.
- Several small defects in one criterion can share one half point.
- A claim supported by its source is not a defect because you would phrase it differently.
- Do not deduct for things the rubric does not ask for.
