# Site profile

Save a filled copy as `blog-council.profile.md` in your project. The council reads it every round. Keep it short and concrete: rules the reviewers can check.

## The site
- **Site and section:** e.g. example.com/blog
- **Audience:** who reads it, where, at what reading level
- **Language and spelling:** e.g. British English; terms in the local language explained on first use
- **Byline:** the author name every article carries

## The call to action
- **What the article should lead to:** e.g. a free trial, a newsletter, a product page
- **The product page(s), to quote from:** URLs
- **What may be said about it:** e.g. "the first lesson is free" (and nothing broader)

## Hard rules (break one and the article fails)
- e.g. never claim a feature the product page does not state
- e.g. never write words for a real person, not even as a placeholder
- e.g. never link to unlicensed copies
- e.g. no traffic, ranking or sales numbers

## Banned words and phrases
- e.g. "ultimate", "best ever", "guaranteed"

## Sensitive topics
- e.g. religion or health: say clearly when a point is the source's and when it is later tradition or opinion; read by a person who answers for the site before publishing

## Images
- **The theme crops the cover to:** e.g. a 16:7 banner from the middle of the image
- **Allowed sources:** own art; openly licensed with credit

## Quality bar
- **Stop rule:** two consecutive clean rounds at or above **9.0** (raise if needed)
- **Maximum rounds:** 6
